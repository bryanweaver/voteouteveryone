## Naming Strategy Considerations

### ✅ Good Characteristics:
- **Short** (easier to remember, type, share)
- **Clear** (mission obvious from name)
- **Positive** (focus on "new" vs just "no")
- **Nonpartisan sounding** (avoid political connotations)
- **Memorable** (easy to recall at voting booth)
- **Pronounceable** (for word-of-mouth)

### ⚠️ Avoid:
- Partisan-sounding words (resist, revolt, rebel)
- Negative tone (though "out" is borderline - but clear)
- Too clever/obscure (needs to be instantly understandable)
- Hyphens (harder to communicate verbally)
- Numbers or misspellings (1ncumbent.org = bad)

## Domain Availability Check Recommendations

Before purchasing, check:

1. **.org, .com, .us availability** (try to get matching versions)
2. **Social media handles** (@voteout, @freshballot, etc.)
3. **Trademark search** (USPTO.gov)
4. **Similar domains** (avoid confusion with existing orgs)

### Tools:
- Namecheap.com (domain search)
- Google Domains / Squarespace Domains
- Instant Domain Search (instantdomainsearch.com)
