### The Problem Section

**Headline:** The Incumbent Advantage Problem

**Body Copy:**

In the 2024 elections, **95% of incumbents won reelection**. This isn't because voters are satisfied—Congress has a 20% approval rating. It's because incumbents have overwhelming structural advantages: name recognition, fundraising, institutional resources, and one more thing we can fix: **information asymmetry**.

Most voters don't know which candidates are incumbents, especially in down-ballot races. At the voting booth, they see names and party affiliations, but not who's been in office for years versus who's new.

**We fix that.**

### FAQ Section

**Headline:** Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is this partisan?**
A: No. We're strictly nonpartisan. We identify incumbents from all political parties equally. We make no endorsements or recommendations—we simply make incumbent status visible so you can make informed choices.

**Q: How do you determine who's an incumbent?**
A: We use verified public data from Google Civic Information API, Ballotpedia, and OpenFEC. Incumbent status is factual and publicly available. We cross-reference multiple sources to ensure accuracy.

**Q: What if I want to vote FOR an incumbent?**
A: That's totally fine! Our service doesn't tell you WHO to vote for. We just make it easy to see who IS and ISN'T an incumbent. You make your own choices.

**Q: Do you cover all elections?**
A: We cover federal (President, Senate, House), state (Governor, Legislature, statewide offices), and local (Mayor, Council, School Board, Judges) elections wherever data is available.

**Q: How is this free?**
A: We're funded by grants from foundations that support civic engagement (like Knight Foundation and Democracy Fund) and individual donations from people who believe in the mission. We have no ads and never sell data.

**Q: Is my address private?**
A: Yes. We use your address only to find your ballot. We don't sell, share, or track your data. Addresses are deleted after the election. Our privacy policy is clear and simple.

**Q: What if there are no non-incumbents in a race?**
A: We'll show that clearly on your ballot guide with a note like "All candidates are incumbents" so you're informed about every race.

**Q: Can I get a physical card if I don't have a printer?**
A: Absolutely! We offer free mailing of ballot guide cards. Just enter your mailing address and we'll send it to you before election day at no cost.

**Q: What if I find an error?**
A: Please report it! We have a feedback form on every ballot page. We verify all reports and fix errors immediately.

**Q: Are you trying to vote out all incumbents?**
A: We're not trying to vote out anyone. We provide information. Some voters want to support new candidates, others want to keep incumbents. Our tool serves both by making incumbent status clear.

**Q: Is this legal?**
A: Yes. Providing factual, nonpartisan voter information is protected speech and encouraged in a democracy. We comply with all election laws and include proper disclaimers ("Not authorized by any candidate or political committee").

## Accessibility Notes

### Plain Language Principles
- Use 8th-grade reading level
- Define technical terms (incumbent, down-ballot, etc.)
- Short sentences (under 20 words average)
- Active voice
- Concrete examples

### Screen Reader Considerations
- Proper heading hierarchy (H1 > H2 > H3)
- Alt text for all images
- Descriptive link text ("Learn how we determine incumbent status" not "Click here")
- ARIA labels for icons and symbols

## Tone & Voice Guide

**Overall Tone:** Informative, trustworthy, nonpartisan, approachable

**Do:**
- ✓ Use "we" and "you" (conversational but professional)
- ✓ State facts clearly without spin
- ✓ Explain "why" not just "what"
- ✓ Acknowledge voter autonomy ("you decide")
- ✓ Use specific numbers (95%, 2 minutes, free)

**Don't:**
- ✗ Use partisan language or "us vs. them" framing
- ✗ Make emotional appeals or fear-mongering
- ✗ Oversell or use superlatives ("amazing", "revolutionary")
- ✗ Make promises about election outcomes
- ✗ Use jargon without explanation

**Example Rewrites:**

❌ "Fight back against the corrupt establishment!"
✅ "95% of incumbents won in 2024. See your non-incumbent options."

❌ "Our revolutionary platform will transform democracy!"
✅ "A simple tool to reduce information asymmetry in elections."

❌ "Don't let them win again!"
✅ "Know who's running before you vote."
